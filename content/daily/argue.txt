# @para <id> <cấp 1–3> <emoji> | <chủ đề> | <đề bài / câu hỏi đoạn văn trả lời>
#   mỗi dòng sau là một câu của đoạn, đúng thứ tự, mở đầu bằng vai trò:
#   P (ý chính / câu chủ đề) · E (giải thích) · X (ví dụ) · L (câu kết đoạn, nối về đề)
#   O (ý kiến trái chiều) · R (phản bác)
# ? <loại> câu hỏi || đáp án đúng | sai 1 | sai 2 | sai 3 || giải thích
#   loại: topic (câu chủ đề) · example (ví dụ phù hợp) · weak (lập luận yếu) · hedge (nói giảm, thận trọng)
#         signpost (câu dẫn dắt) · link (câu kết đoạn) · counter (phản biện)

@para p01 1 💻🎓 | Giáo dục · học online | Học trực tuyến có lợi cho sinh viên không?

X For example, a student who lives an hour away from the university can save two hours every day.

@para p02 1 🚲🌿 | Giao thông · xe đạp | Thành phố có nên khuyến khích người dân đi xe đạp?

@para p03 1 📱👨‍👩‍👧 | Công nghệ · gia đình | Điện thoại thông minh ảnh hưởng thế nào đến gia đình?

? topic Đoạn văn nói về lợi ích của việc học ngoại ngữ đối với công việc. Câu chủ đề nào tốt nhất? || Speaking a foreign language can greatly improve a person's job opportunities. | I started learning English when I was ten years old. | There are about 7,000 languages in the world. | English, Chinese and Japanese are popular languages. || Câu chủ đề nêu đúng ý chính (ngoại ngữ → cơ hội việc làm). Các câu khác là thông tin bên lề hoặc chuyện cá nhân.
? topic Đoạn văn giải thích vì sao trẻ em nên chơi thể thao. Câu chủ đề nào tốt nhất? || Playing sports is important for children's physical and social development. | Football is the most popular sport in the world. | My brother plays basketball every Sunday. | Some sports are very expensive. || Câu chủ đề phải trả lời trực tiếp câu hỏi "vì sao" và bao quát cả đoạn.
? topic Câu chủ đề nào quá rộng, KHÔNG phù hợp cho một đoạn văn? || Technology has changed everything in the world. | Online banking has made paying bills much faster. | Video calls help families who live far apart stay in touch. | Electric scooters are becoming popular in big cities. || Câu chủ đề cần đủ hẹp để một đoạn (4–6 câu) chứng minh được. "Changed everything" quá rộng.
? topic Câu nào là câu chủ đề (không phải câu ví dụ)? || Working from home can reduce stress for many employees. | Last year, my aunt started working from home three days a week. | She no longer spends two hours in traffic jams. | Now she has time to cook breakfast for her children. || Câu chủ đề nêu ý chung; ba câu còn lại là chi tiết / ví dụ cụ thể.
? example Ý chính: "Social media helps small businesses reach more customers." Ví dụ nào phù hợp nhất? || A small bakery in Da Nang doubled its orders after posting photos of its cakes on Facebook. | Facebook was created in 2004 by Mark Zuckerberg. | Many teenagers spend hours on TikTok every day. | Some people think social media is a waste of time. || Ví dụ phải chứng minh đúng ý chính: doanh nghiệp nhỏ + nhiều khách hơn nhờ mạng xã hội.
? example Ý chính: "Living in a big city can be very expensive." Ví dụ nào phù hợp nhất? || In central Hanoi, renting a small flat can cost half of a young worker's salary. | Hanoi has a population of more than eight million people. | Big cities have many shopping centres and restaurants. | Traffic in big cities is often terrible. || Ví dụ phải nói về chi phí đắt đỏ; các câu khác đúng nhưng không chứng minh ý này.
? example Ý chính: "Regular exercise improves mental health." Ví dụ nào hỗ trợ tốt nhất? || Studies show that people who walk for thirty minutes a day feel less anxious. | Many gyms open at five o'clock in the morning. | Running shoes can be very expensive. | Some professional athletes earn millions of dollars. || Chỉ câu đầu nối exercise với sức khỏe tinh thần (less anxious).
? example Ý chính: "Public libraries are still useful today." Ví dụ nào phù hợp nhất? || Many students without a quiet room at home go to the library to study for exams. | The first public library in the world opened a long time ago. | Some libraries are very old and beautiful buildings. | People can read e-books on their phones. || Ví dụ cần cho thấy thư viện vẫn có ích hiện nay.
? link Đoạn văn: ý chính là "Cycling to work saves money", có giải thích và ví dụ. Câu kết đoạn nào phù hợp nhất? || Therefore, cycling is a practical choice for people who want to cut their transport costs. | Some bicycles are very expensive. | In conclusion, cars are dangerous. | I really like riding my bike at weekends. || Câu kết đoạn nhắc lại ý chính và nối về đề (tiết kiệm tiền), không đưa ý mới.
? link Câu nào là câu kết đoạn tốt cho đoạn văn về "lợi ích của việc đọc sách"? || In short, reading regularly benefits both our knowledge and our wellbeing. | Firstly, reading helps us learn new words. | For example, my sister reads two books a month. | Some people prefer watching films to reading. || Câu kết đoạn tóm lại ý chính; "Firstly" mở ý, "For example" là ví dụ.
? signpost Bạn muốn mở ý thứ hai trong bài. Câu nào phù hợp? || Another reason why people choose to live in cities is the quality of healthcare. | In conclusion, people live in cities. | For example, hospitals are good. | Although cities are big. || "Another reason why…" là câu dẫn dắt mở ý tiếp theo, đầy đủ và rõ ràng.
? signpost Câu nào nêu rõ quan điểm của người viết ngay đầu bài? || In my opinion, the advantages of online shopping clearly outweigh the disadvantages. | Online shopping is shopping on the internet. | Many people talk about online shopping. | Is online shopping good or bad? || Mở bài nên nêu quan điểm rõ ràng (In my opinion / I believe that…), không chỉ định nghĩa hay đặt câu hỏi.
? weak Lập luận nào yếu nhất? || Everyone in my class likes video games, so all young people love them. | Many surveys show that a large number of teenagers play video games regularly. | Video games can improve reaction speed, according to some research. | Some young people prefer sports to video games. || Lỗi khái quát hoá: từ một lớp học suy ra "tất cả người trẻ".
? weak Câu nào thiếu giải thích, chỉ khẳng định? || Uniforms are good because they are good. | Uniforms are good because students do not need to worry about what to wear. | Uniforms can reduce the pressure to buy expensive clothes. | Uniforms help students feel part of the school community. || Lập luận vòng tròn: lấy kết luận làm lý do.
? hedge Câu nào phù hợp hơn với văn viết học thuật? || Fast food may contribute to health problems such as obesity. | Fast food kills everyone who eats it. | Fast food is 100% bad. | Fast food is super terrible for you. || Văn học thuật tránh nói tuyệt đối; "may contribute to" thận trọng và chính xác hơn.
? hedge Câu nào nói giảm hợp lý nhất? || Young people tend to spend more time online than older people. | All young people are always online. | Young people never go outside. | Every teenager uses the internet 24 hours a day. || "tend to" = có xu hướng — tránh khái quát tuyệt đối (all, always, never, every).
? topic Đề: "Some people think students should do part-time jobs." Câu chủ đề nào cho đoạn ủng hộ? || Doing a part-time job can help students develop useful skills for their future careers. | I have never had a part-time job. | Part-time jobs are jobs that are not full-time. | Some students are very busy. || Câu chủ đề nêu một lý do ủng hộ cụ thể (kỹ năng cho sự nghiệp).
? example Ý chính: "Tourism can damage the environment." Ví dụ nào phù hợp nhất? || On some popular beaches, plastic bottles left by tourists harm sea turtles and fish. | Many tourists like taking photos. | Tourism is a big industry in Thailand. | Hotels near the beach are expensive. || Ví dụ chứng minh tác hại môi trường cụ thể.
? signpost Bạn muốn đưa một ví dụ. Câu dẫn dắt nào đúng? || A good example of this is Singapore, which has very clean streets. | Such as Singapore has clean streets. | For example of Singapore, the streets are clean. | Singapore, for example of this, is clean. || "A good example of this is…" / "For example, …" là cách đưa ví dụ đúng; such as không đứng đầu câu.
? link Câu nào nối lại về đề bài "Should children learn to cook at school?" tốt nhất? || For these reasons, cooking lessons should be part of the school curriculum. | Cooking is fun. | My mother is a very good cook. | Many restaurants are open late at night. || Câu kết nối về đề: trả lời "should" bằng một khuyến nghị rõ ràng.

@para p12 2 🧒📺 | Trẻ em · màn hình | Trẻ em xem màn hình quá nhiều có hại không?

X According to some doctors, screen time is one of the reasons why more children are overweight today.

@para p13 2 🚗💨 | Môi trường · xe hơi | Có nên đánh thuế cao xe hơi cá nhân?

X When London introduced a charge for driving into the city centre, traffic in that area fell considerably.

@para p14 2 🎓💼 | Giáo dục · đại học | Có phải ai cũng nên học đại học?

L Young people should therefore be encouraged to consider all their options, not only university.

@para p15 2 🛒🌐 | Mua sắm · online | Mua sắm online ảnh hưởng gì đến cửa hàng nhỏ?

L To survive, local shops may need to offer something that websites cannot, such as personal service.

@para p16 2 🧓🏥 | Xã hội · dân số già | Dân số già hoá đặt ra thách thức gì?

X In Japan, where more than a quarter of the population is over sixty-five, health spending has risen steadily for decades.
L Governments will therefore need to plan carefully to keep healthcare affordable.

@para p17 2 📰📱 | Truyền thông · tin tức | Đọc tin trên mạng xã hội có đáng tin?

X During health crises, for example, fake cures have been shared millions of times before experts could correct them.
L Readers should therefore check important news with trusted sources before believing or sharing it.

L Video games, if played in moderation, can therefore be more than just entertainment.

@para p20 2 💧🏭 | Môi trường · nước sạch | Ai chịu trách nhiệm bảo vệ nguồn nước?

@para p21 3 🏫📵 | Giáo dục · điện thoại trong lớp | Có nên cấm điện thoại trong trường học?
O Some people argue that mobile phones should be banned in schools completely.
E They believe phones distract students and make it harder for teachers to control the class.
R However, a complete ban may ignore the fact that phones can be useful learning tools.
X Students can use them to look up new words, check facts or join online quizzes during lessons.
L A better solution, therefore, may be to allow phones only for specific learning activities.

@para p22 3 🧪🐒 | Khoa học · thử nghiệm trên động vật | Có nên cấm thử nghiệm trên động vật?
O It is often claimed that testing medicines on animals is necessary to protect human health.
E Supporters say that new drugs must be tested on living bodies before they can be given to people safely.
R Nevertheless, modern alternatives now make many animal tests unnecessary.
X Scientists can increasingly use computer models and human cells grown in laboratories to check whether a drug is safe.
L Animal testing should therefore be reduced as far as possible and replaced wherever alternatives exist.

@para p23 3 💰🎓 | Giáo dục · học phí | Đại học có nên miễn học phí?
O Opponents of free university education point out that it would be very expensive for taxpayers.
E They argue that public money could be spent on more urgent needs, such as hospitals.
R Admittedly, the cost is high; however, educated citizens tend to earn more and pay more tax later in life.
X Countries such as Norway, where university is free, have some of the most skilled workforces in the world.
L In the long term, free higher education may therefore be an investment rather than a cost.

@para p24 3 🤖👷 | Công nghệ · trí tuệ nhân tạo | AI có lấy mất việc làm của con người?
O Many people fear that artificial intelligence will take away millions of jobs.
E It is true that machines can now do tasks such as answering customer questions or checking documents faster than humans.
R However, history suggests that new technology also creates new types of work.
X When computers became common, jobs such as web designer and data analyst appeared that had not existed before.
L Rather than fearing AI, societies should focus on training workers for the jobs it will create.

@para p25 3 🏟️💸 | Xã hội · tiền cho thể thao | Chính phủ có nên chi nhiều tiền cho thể thao chuyên nghiệp?
O Some believe that governments should spend large sums on professional sport because it brings national pride.
E When a national team wins, people often feel happier and more united.
R On the other hand, this money might be used more effectively to encourage ordinary people to exercise.
X Building free sports facilities in local parks, for example, could benefit thousands of people rather than a few athletes.
L Funding for grassroots sport should therefore be a higher priority than funding for elite teams.

@para p26 3 🌍✈️ | Môi trường · đi máy bay | Có nên hạn chế đi máy bay vì môi trường?
O It could be argued that people should fly less in order to protect the environment.
E Air travel produces a large amount of carbon dioxide per passenger compared with trains.
R Even so, simply asking people to fly less is unlikely to work unless there are cheap alternatives.
X In Europe, where fast trains connect many cities, more travellers now choose the train for short journeys.
L Investing in high-speed rail may therefore be more effective than telling people not to fly.

@para p27 3 🧑‍🏫💻 | Giáo dục · giáo viên và máy tính | Máy tính có thể thay thế giáo viên?
O Some people predict that computers will one day replace teachers.
E Online courses can already deliver lessons to millions of learners at a very low cost.
R Nonetheless, teachers do much more than deliver information.
X They notice when a student is confused or unhappy and can change the lesson or offer support immediately.
L For this reason, technology is more likely to support teachers than to replace them.

@para p28 3 🏘️🏗️ | Đô thị · nhà cao tầng | Thành phố có nên xây thêm nhà cao tầng?
O Supporters of high-rise buildings argue that they are the best answer to housing shortages in crowded cities.
E Tall buildings can house many families on a small piece of land.
R However, if they are not well planned, high-rise areas may lack green space and community facilities.
X Some tower blocks built quickly in the past are now known for social problems and poor living conditions.
L High-rise housing can therefore be part of the solution, but only if it is carefully designed.

@para p29 3 🍽️🌱 | Sức khỏe · ăn chay | Mọi người có nên ăn ít thịt hơn?
O Many argue that people should eat less meat for health and environmental reasons.
E Producing meat uses a lot of land and water, and eating too much red meat is linked to some diseases.
R That said, meat is an important source of protein and iron, especially for people with limited diets.
X In poorer rural areas, a small amount of meat may be the most affordable way to get these nutrients.
L A balanced approach, in which people reduce rather than give up meat, therefore seems most realistic.

@para p30 3 👩‍💼👨‍🍼 | Xã hội · nghỉ thai sản cho bố | Có nên cho người bố nghỉ dài ngày khi có con?
O Some employers claim that long paid leave for new fathers is too costly for businesses.
E They worry that small companies cannot afford to pay staff who are not working.
R Nevertheless, there is growing evidence that such leave brings long-term benefits.
X In Sweden, where fathers share parental leave, mothers return to work more easily and children spend more time with both parents.
L With some government support for small firms, paternity leave could therefore benefit families and the economy.
